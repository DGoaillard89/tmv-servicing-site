How we use the information you send us through this website. Short and to the point.

Who we are

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, postcode and whatever you tell us about your enquiry. We don't ask for anything else.

Why we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, arrange and carry out the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. We don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it's stored

Enquiries are passed securely through Cloudflare and stored in our customer records system, Airtable. These providers process data on our behalf under their own data protection terms.

Cookies and similar technologies

We only use analytics and advertising cookies if you say yes. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you're happy for us to use:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics, so we can count visits and see which pages are useful.
  • Advertising: Google Ads, so we can measure whether our adverts lead to enquiries and show our ads to people who've visited this site before.

Both are loaded through Google Tag Manager, and none of them load until you accept. If you reject, or ignore the banner, they stay off. Your choice is saved in your browser (local storage) so we don't ask on every page. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. Google explains how it uses information from sites like ours at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you reach us from an advert or a tagged link, the web address can include campaign tags (such as a Google click ID or "utm" labels). We add those to your enquiry so we know which advert or link brought you here. With your consent, they're also kept in your browser for the rest of that visit.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with them, as it does for most websites.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a customer, we delete enquiry details within 24 months. Customer records are kept for as long as we need them for legal, insurance and compliance reasons.
