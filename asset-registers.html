The register

One list of every valve you own

On the first visit we survey the site and record each TMV, including the awkward ones behind bath panels and in ceiling voids. Each valve gets a unique ID tag so it can be matched to its records for years to come.

After that, every test result goes against the right valve. You can see at a glance which ones passed, which needed work and which are due next.

What's recorded

What we keep for each valve

Where it is

Building, floor, room and outlet, plus a note on how to get to it if it's hidden.

What it is

Make, model, TMV2 or TMV3, and the outlet it serves: bath, shower, basin or bidet.

The readings

Hot and cold supply temperatures, the mixed temperature and the set point.

Fail-safe result

Whether the valve shut down properly when the cold supply was isolated.

Work done

Cleaned, descaled, recalibrated, cartridge replaced or valve swapped out.

When it's next due

Based on your agreed interval and how stable that valve has been.

Scheduling

We book the visits, so you don't have to remember

Once your interval is agreed, we plan the next visit and get in touch ahead of time to confirm a date. If you've got several sites, we group visits sensibly so engineers aren't zig-zagging across the country.

Reminders

A nudge before anything falls due

  • A reminder ahead of each due date
  • A follow-up on any outstanding remedial work
  • Early checks for newly installed valves, so they're not forgotten
Why it matters

Good records protect you

If there's ever an incident, the first question will be "when was that valve last tested, and what did it show?" A clear register answers that in seconds. It also helps with inspections, insurance and handovers when staff change.

FAQs

Questions about asset registers

We've never had a register. Where do we start?

With a survey. On the first visit we find and tag every TMV, and usually service them at the same time. From then on you've got a complete list to work from.

Can you take over an existing register?

Yes. Send us what you've got. We'll check it on site, fill the gaps and carry on from there so your history isn't lost.

What format will the register be in?

A clear document you can keep with your water safety records and share with your risk assessor or auditor. If you need it laid out a certain way for your own systems, just ask.
